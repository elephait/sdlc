# Evidence-based research

Follow `universal.instructions.md`.

1. Retrieve and read primary sources rather than relying on search snippets.
2. Verify version-sensitive facts against the product and version actually in
   use. Include retrieval dates for prices, plan limits, and mutable policies.
3. Support load-bearing claims with two independent authoritative sources when
   practical. State conflicts and distinguish directly verified evidence from
   claims merely reported by a source.
4. Put unsupported or unavailable facts in a gaps list. A failed retrieval is a
   tool limitation, not evidence that the fact or resource does not exist.
5. Prefer raw evidence, reproducible commands, source code, specifications,
   measured results, and externally checkable acceptance criteria.
6. Do not invent numbers or upgrade a pilot, tabletop review, or vendor claim
   into production certification.

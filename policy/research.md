# Research policy

Use when external facts materially affect a software decision.

- Read primary evidence for the actual product and version: source, specifications, official documentation or measured results. Search snippets are leads, not verification.
- Cite load-bearing claims. Cross-check contested or high-impact claims when practical; do not require redundant sources for straightforward documented behavior.
- Separate observed facts, source claims and inference. Date volatile information such as prices and limits. State contradictions and missing evidence without inventing certainty.
- Try another retrieval route when useful; a failed fetch does not prove absence. Never present a vendor claim, tabletop exercise or pilot as production certification.
